About this accessible edition

What this edition contains

This complete accessible English edition includes all 79 planned reader items. Its 78 mathematical chapters contain 34,391 frozen-census formula occurrences and 3,020 formal objects. The HTML edition passed independent static and silent runtime acceptance. The synchronized EPUB 3.3 edition passed EPUBCheck, Ace by DAISY, independent source-and-anchor replay, and silent archived-book runtime acceptance.

The equation guides contain 14,610 chapter entries representing 10,348 stable census expression identifiers across all 78 mathematical chapters. 501 source-generated math objects and five complete source-composite formulas are recorded separately. First-order Axiomatic Deduction contributes 209 entries; First-order Tableaux contributes 275, First-order Natural Deduction contributes 279, and First-order Sequent Calculus contributes 394; First-order Completeness contributes 309; Beyond First-order Logic contributes 152; Basics of Model Theory contributes 223; Models of Arithmetic contributes 343.

The source is licensed under CC BY 4.0. This adaptation preserves attribution and immutable source files.

Accessibility design

  • Read uses headings, landmarks, exact source links, and native unflattened MathML.
  • Listen / words only follows source order and replaces every formal occurrence, truth table, proof tree, tableau, and derivation with reviewed words.
  • Explore exposes every expression, formal object, occurrence, and resolved reference.
  • Source and provenance preserves line-addressable source. The 70 accepted-slice chapters include byte-exact accepted projected slices and exact source-coordinate crosswalks; their crosswalks are not represented as complete copies of every upstream source file.

Limitations

No prerecorded audio is included. This edition has not been tested hands-on with every screen reader, braille display, voice-control system, dedicated magnifier, or speech engine.
