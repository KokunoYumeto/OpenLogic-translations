This part contains material on classical propositional logic. The first chapter is relatively rudimentary and just lists definitions and results, many proofs are not carried out but are left as exercises. The material on proof systems and the completeness theorem is included from the part on first-order logic, with the “F O L” tag set to false. This leaves out everything related to predicates, terms, and quantifiers, and replaces talk of structures capital M with talk about valuations vee.

It is planned to expand this part to include more detail, and to add further topics and results, such as truth-functional completeness.
