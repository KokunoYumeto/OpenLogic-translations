  25. assumptions~$\Gamma$ is a finite tree of !!{sentence}s satisfying the
  26. following conditions:
  27. \begin{enumerate}
  28. \item The topmost !!{sentence}s of the tree are either in $\Gamma$ or
  29. are !!{discharged} by an inference in the tree.
  30. \item The bottommost !!{sentence} of the tree is~$!A$.
  31. \item Every !!{sentence} in the tree except the sentence~$!A$ at
  32. the bottom is a premise of a correct application of an inference
  33. rule whose conclusion stands directly below that !!{sentence} in the
  34. tree.
  35. \end{enumerate}

  37. and $\Gamma$ its !!{undischarged} assumptions.

  40. \emph{!!{derivable}} from~$\Gamma$, or in symbols: $\Gamma \Proves
  41. !A$. If there is !!a{derivation} of~$!A$ in which every assumption is
  42. !!{discharged}, we write~$\Proves !A$.
  43. \end{defn}

  72. \AxiomC{$\Discharge{\lnot !A \lor !B}{1}$}
  73. \DeduceC{$!A \lif !B$}
  74. \DischargeRule{\Intro{\lif}}{1}
  75. \UnaryInfC{$(\lnot !A \lor !B) \lif (!A \lif !B)$}
  76. \end{prooftree}
  77. This leaves us with two possibilities to continue. Either we can
  78. keep working from the bottom up and look for another application
  79. of the \Intro{\lif} rule, or we can work from the top down and apply a
  80. \Elim{\lor} rule. Let us apply the latter. We will use the assumption
  81. $\lnot !A \lor !B$ as the leftmost premise of \Elim{\lor}. For a valid
  82. application of \Elim{\lor}, the other two premises must be identical
  83. to the conclusion $!A \lif !B$, but each may be derived in turn from
  84. another assumption, namely one of the two disjuncts of $\lnot !A \lor !B$.
  85. So our !!{derivation} will look like this:
  86. \begin{prooftree}
  87. \AxiomC{$\Discharge{\lnot !A \lor !B}{1}$}
  88. \AxiomC{$\Discharge{\lnot !A}{2}$}
  89. \DeduceC{$!A \lif !B$}
  90. \AxiomC{$\Discharge{!B}{2}$}
  91. \DeduceC{$!A \lif !B$}
  92. \DischargeRule{\Elim{\lor}}{2}
  93. \TrinaryInfC{$!A \lif !B$}
  94. \DischargeRule{\Intro{\lif}}{1}
  95. \UnaryInfC{$(\lnot !A \lor !B) \lif (!A \lif !B)$}
  96. \end{prooftree}
  98. In each of the two branches on the right, we want to !!{derive} $!A
  99. \lif !B$, which is best done using \Intro{\lif}.
  100. \begin{prooftree}
  101. \AxiomC{$\Discharge{\lnot !A \lor !B}{1}$}
  102. \AxiomC{$\Discharge{\lnot !A}{2}, \Discharge{!A}{3}$}
  103. \DeduceC{$!B$}
  104. \DischargeRule{\Intro{\lif}}{3}
  105. \UnaryInfC{$!A \lif !B$}
  106. \AxiomC{$\Discharge{!B}{2}, \Discharge{!A}{4}$}
  107. \DeduceC{$!B$}
  108. \DischargeRule{\Intro{\lif}}{4}
  109. \UnaryInfC{$!A \lif !B$}
  110. \DischargeRule{\Elim{\lor}}{2}
  111. \TrinaryInfC{$!A \lif !B$}
  112. \DischargeRule{\Intro{\lif}}{1}
  113. \UnaryInfC{$(\lnot !A \lor !B) \lif (!A \lif !B)$}
  114. \end{prooftree}
  116. For the two missing parts of the !!{derivation}, we need
  117. !!{derivation}s of $!B$ from $\lnot !A$ and $!A$ in the middle, and
  118. from $!A$ and $!B$ on the left. Let's take the former first. $\lnot
  119. !A$ and $!A$ are the two premises of \Elim{\lnot}:
  120. \begin{prooftree}
  121. \AxiomC{$\Discharge{\lnot !A}{2}$}
  122. \AxiomC{$\Discharge{!A}{3}$}
  123. \RightLabel{\Elim{\lnot}}
  124. \BinaryInfC{$\lfalse$}
  125. \DeduceC{$!B$}
  126. \end{prooftree}
  127. By using \FalseInt, we can obtain $!B$ as a conclusion and
  128. complete the branch.
  129. \begin{prooftree}
  130. \AxiomC{$\Discharge{\lnot !A \lor !B}{1}$}
  131. \AxiomC{$\Discharge{\lnot !A}{2}$}
  132. \AxiomC{$\Discharge{!A}{3}$}
  133. \RightLabel{\Intro{\lfalse}}
  134. \BinaryInfC{$\lfalse$}
  135. \RightLabel{\FalseInt}
  136. \UnaryInfC{$!B$}
  137. \DischargeRule{\Intro{\lif}}{3}
  138. \UnaryInfC{$!A \lif !B$}
  139. \AxiomC{$\Discharge{!B}{2}, \Discharge{!A}{4}$}
  140. \DeduceC{$!B$}
  141. \DischargeRule{\Intro{\lif}}{4}
  142. \UnaryInfC{$!A \lif !B$}
  143. \DischargeRule{\Elim{\lor}}{2}
  144. \TrinaryInfC{$!A \lif !B$}
  145. \DischargeRule{\Intro{\lif}}{1}
  146. \UnaryInfC{$(\lnot !A \lor !B) \lif (!A \lif !B)$}
  147. \end{prooftree}
  149. Let's now look at the rightmost branch. Here it's important to
  150. realize that the definition of !!{derivation} \emph{allows assumptions
  151. to be discharged} but \emph{does not require} them to be. In other
  152. words, if we can derive $!B$ from one of the assumptions $!A$ and $!B$
  153. without using the other, that's ok. And to !!{derive} $!B$ from~$!B$
  154. is trivial: $!B$ by itself is such !!a{derivation}, and no inferences
  155. are needed. So we can simply delete the assumption~$!A$.
  156. \begin{prooftree}
  157. \AxiomC{$\Discharge{\lnot !A \lor !B}{1}$}
  158. \AxiomC{$\Discharge{\lnot !A}{2}$}
  159. \AxiomC{$\Discharge{!A}{3}$}
  160. \RightLabel{\Elim{\lnot}}
  161. \BinaryInfC{$\lfalse$}
  162. \RightLabel{\FalseInt}
  163. \UnaryInfC{$!B$}
  164. \DischargeRule{\Intro{\lif}}{3}
  165. \UnaryInfC{$!A \lif !B$}
  166. \AxiomC{$\Discharge{!B}{2}$}
  167. \RightLabel{\Intro{\lif}}
  168. \UnaryInfC{$!A \lif !B$}
  169. \DischargeRule{\Elim{\lor}}{2}
  170. \TrinaryInfC{$!A \lif !B$}
  171. \DischargeRule{\Intro{\lif}}{1}
  172. \UnaryInfC{$(\lnot !A \lor !B) \lif (!A \lif !B)$}
  173. \end{prooftree}
  174. Note that in the finished !!{derivation}, the rightmost \Intro{\lif}
  175. inference does not actually discharge any assumptions.
  176. \end{ex}
  178. \begin{ex}
  179. So far we have not needed the \FalseCl{} rule. It is special in that
  180. it allows us to discharge an assumption that isn't a sub-!!{formula} of
  181. the conclusion of the rule. It is closely related to the \FalseInt{}
  182. rule. In fact, the \FalseInt{} rule is a special case of the
  183. \FalseCl{} rule---there is a logic called ``intuitionistic logic'' in
  184. which only \FalseInt{} is allowed. The \FalseCl{} rule is a last
  185. resort when nothing else works. For instance, suppose we want to
  186. !!{derive} $!A \lor \lnot !A$. Our usual strategy would be to attempt
  187. to !!{derive} $!A \lor \lnot !A$ using $\Intro{\lor}$. But this would
  188. require us to !!{derive} either $!A$ or $\lnot !A$ from no
  189. assumptions, and this can't be done. \FalseCl{} to the rescue!
  190. \begin{prooftree}
  191. \AxiomC{$\Discharge{\lnot(!A \lor \lnot !A)}{1}$}
  192. \DeduceC{$\lfalse$}
  193. \DischargeRule{\FalseCl}{1}
  194. \UnaryInfC{$!A \lor \lnot !A$}
  195. \end{prooftree}
  196. Now we're looking for !!a{derivation} of $\lfalse$ from $\lnot(!A \lor
  197. \lnot !A)$. Since $\lfalse$ is the conclusion of $\Elim{\lnot}$ we
  198. might try that:
  199. \begin{prooftree}
  200. \AxiomC{$\Discharge{\lnot(!A \lor \lnot !A)}{1}$}
  201. \DeduceC{$\lnot !A$}
  202. \AxiomC{$\Discharge{\lnot(!A \lor \lnot !A)}{1}$}
  203. \DeduceC{$!A$}
  204. \RightLabel{\Elim{\lnot}}
  205. \BinaryInfC{$\lfalse$}
  206. \DischargeRule{\FalseCl}{1}
  207. \UnaryInfC{$!A \lor \lnot !A$}
  208. \end{prooftree}
  209. Our strategy for finding !!a{derivation} of~$\lnot !A$ calls for an
  210. application of~$\Intro{\lnot}$:
  211. \begin{prooftree}
  212. \AxiomC{$\Discharge{\lnot(!A \lor \lnot !A)}{1}, \Discharge{!A}{2}$}
  213. \DeduceC{$\lfalse$}
  214. \DischargeRule{\Intro{\lnot}}{2}
  215. \UnaryInfC{$\lnot !A$}
  216. \AxiomC{$\Discharge{\lnot(!A \lor \lnot !A)}{1}$}
  217. \DeduceC{$!A$}
  218. \RightLabel{\Elim{\lnot}}
  219. \BinaryInfC{$\lfalse$}
  220. \DischargeRule{\FalseCl}{1}
  221. \UnaryInfC{$!A \lor \lnot !A$}
  222. \end{prooftree}
  223. Here, we can get $\lfalse$ easily by applying $\Elim{\lnot}$ to the
  224. assumption $\lnot(!A \lor \lnot !A)$ and $!A \lor \lnot !A$ which
  225. follows from our new assumption $!A$ by~$\Intro{\lor}$:
  226. \begin{prooftree}
  227. \AxiomC{$\Discharge{\lnot(!A \lor \lnot !A)}{1}$}
  228. \AxiomC{$\Discharge{!A}{2}$}
  229. \RightLabel{\Intro{\lor}}
  230. \UnaryInfC{$!A \lor \lnot !A$}
  231. \RightLabel{\Elim{\lnot}}
  232. \BinaryInfC{$\lfalse$}
  233. \DischargeRule{\Intro{\lnot}}{2}
  234. \UnaryInfC{$\lnot !A$}
  235. \AxiomC{$\Discharge{\lnot(!A \lor \lnot !A)}{1}$}
  236. \DeduceC{$!A$}
  237. \RightLabel{\Elim{\lnot}}
  238. \BinaryInfC{$\lfalse$}
  239. \DischargeRule{\FalseCl}{1}
  240. \UnaryInfC{$!A \lor \lnot !A$}
  241. \end{prooftree}
  242. On the right side we use the same strategy, except we get $!A$ by~\FalseCl:
  243. \begin{prooftree}
  244. \AxiomC{$\Discharge{\lnot(!A \lor \lnot !A)}{1}$}
  245. \AxiomC{$\Discharge{!A}{2}$}
  246. \RightLabel{\Intro{\lor}}
  247. \UnaryInfC{$!A \lor \lnot !A$}
  248. \RightLabel{\Elim{\lnot}}
  249. \BinaryInfC{$\lfalse$}
  250. \DischargeRule{\Intro{\lnot}}{2}
  251. \UnaryInfC{$\lnot !A$}
  252. \AxiomC{$\Discharge{\lnot(!A \lor \lnot !A)}{1}$}
  253. \AxiomC{$\Discharge{\lnot !A}{3}$}
  254. \RightLabel{\Intro{\lor}}
  255. \UnaryInfC{$!A \lor \lnot !A$}
  256. \RightLabel{\Elim{\lnot}}
  257. \BinaryInfC{$\lfalse$}
  258. \DischargeRule{\FalseCl}{3}
  259. \UnaryInfC{$!A$}
  260. \RightLabel{\Elim{\lnot}}
  261. \BinaryInfC{$\lfalse$}
  262. \DischargeRule{\FalseCl}{1}
  263. \UnaryInfC{$!A \lor \lnot !A$}
  264. \end{prooftree}
  265. \end{ex}

  13. \begin{ex}
  14. When dealing with quantifiers, we have to make sure not to violate the
  15. eigenvariable condition, and sometimes this requires us to play around
  16. with the order of carrying out certain inferences. In general, it
  17. helps to try and take care of rules subject to the eigenvariable
  18. condition first (they will be lower down in the finished proof).
  20. Let's see how we'd give !!a{derivation} of the !!{formula}
  21. $\lexists[x][\lnot !A(x)] \lif \lnot \lforall[x][!A(x)]$.
  22. Starting as usual, we write
  23. \begin{prooftree}
  24. \AxiomC{}
  25. \UnaryInfC{$\lexists[x][\lnot !A(x)]\lif \lnot \lforall[x][!A(x)]$}
  26. \end{prooftree}
  27. We start by writing down what it would take to justify that last step
  28. using the \Intro{\lif} rule.
  29. \begin{prooftree}
  30. \AxiomC{$\Discharge{\lexists[x][\lnot !A(x)]}{1}$}
  31. \DeduceC{$\lnot \lforall[x][!A(x)]$}
  32. \DischargeRule{\Intro{\lif}}{1}
  33. \UnaryInfC{$\lexists[x][\lnot !A(x)]\lif \lnot \lforall[x][!A(x)]$}
  34. \end{prooftree}
  35. Since there is no obvious rule to apply to $\lnot \lforall[x][!A(x)]$,
  36. we will proceed by setting up the !!{derivation} so we can use the
  37. \Elim{\lexists} rule. Here we must pay attention to the eigenvariable
  38. condition, and choose a constant that does not appear in
  39. $\lexists[x][!A(x)]$ or any assumptions that it depends on.
  40. (Since no !!{constant}s appear, however, any choice will do fine.)
  41. \begin{prooftree}
  42. \AxiomC{$\Discharge{\lexists[x][\lnot !A(x)]}{1}$}
  43. \AxiomC{$\Discharge{\lnot !A(a)}{2}$}
  44. \DeduceC{$\lnot \lforall[x][!A(x)]$}
  45. \DischargeRule{\Elim{\lexists}}{2}
  46. \BinaryInfC{$\lnot \lforall[x][!A(x)]$}
  47. \DischargeRule{\Intro{\lif}}{1}
  48. \UnaryInfC{$\lexists[x][\lnot !A(x)] \lif \lnot \lforall[x][!A(x)]$}
  49. \end{prooftree}
  50. In order to derive $\lnot \lforall[x][!A(x)]$, we will attempt to use
  51. the \Intro{\lnot} rule: this requires that we derive a contradiction,
  52. possibly using $\lforall[x][!A(x)]$ as an additional assumption. Of
  53. course, this contradiction may involve the assumption $\lnot !A(a)$
  54. which will be discharged by the \Elim{\lexists} inference. We can set it
  55. up as follows:
  56. \begin{prooftree}
  57. \AxiomC{$\Discharge{\lexists[x][\lnot !A(x)]}{1}$}
  58. \AxiomC{$\Discharge{\lnot !A(a)}{2}, \Discharge{\lforall[x][!A(x)]}{3}$}
  59. \DeduceC{$\lfalse$}
  60. \DischargeRule{\Intro{\lnot}}{3}
  61. \UnaryInfC{$\lnot \lforall[x][!A(x)]$}
  62. \DischargeRule{\Elim{\lexists}}{2}
  63. \BinaryInfC{$\lnot \lforall[x][!A(x)]$}
  64. \DischargeRule{\Intro{\lif}}{1}
  65. \UnaryInfC{$\lexists[x][\lnot !A(x)]\lif \lnot \lforall[x][!A(x)]$}
  66. \end{prooftree}
  67. It looks like we are close to getting a contradiction. The easiest
  68. rule to apply is the \Elim{\lforall}, which has no eigenvariable
  69. conditions. Since we can use any term we want to replace the
  70. universally quantified~$x$, it makes the most sense to continue
  71. using~$a$ so we can reach a contradiction.
  72. \begin{prooftree}
  73. \AxiomC{$\Discharge{\lexists[x][\lnot !A(x)]}{1}$}
  74. \AxiomC{$\Discharge{\lnot !A(a)}{2}$}
  75. \AxiomC{$\Discharge{\lforall[x][!A(x)]}{3}$}
  76. \RightLabel{\Elim{\lforall}}
  77. \UnaryInfC{$!A(a)$}
  78. \RightLabel{\Elim{\lnot}}
  79. \BinaryInfC{$\lfalse$}
  80. \DischargeRule{\Intro{\lnot}}{3}
  81. \UnaryInfC{$\lnot \lforall[x][!A(x)]$}
  82. \DischargeRule{\Elim{\lexists}}{2}
  83. \BinaryInfC{$\lnot \lforall[x][!A(x)]$}
  84. \DischargeRule{\Intro{\lif}}{1}
  85. \UnaryInfC{$\lexists[x][\lnot !A(x)]\lif \lnot \lforall[x][!A(x)]$}
  86. \end{prooftree}
  88. It is important, especially when dealing with quantifiers, to double
  89. check at this point that the eigenvariable condition has not been
  90. violated. Since the only rule we applied that is subject to the
  91. eigenvariable condition was \Elim{\exists}, and the eigenvariable~$a$
  92. does not occur in any assumptions it depends on, this is a
  93. correct !!{derivation}.
  94. \end{ex}
  96. \begin{ex}
  97. Sometimes we may derive !!a{formula} from other !!{formula}s.
  98. In these cases, we may have undischarged assumptions. It is
  99. important to keep track of our assumptions as well
  100. as the end goal.
  102. Let's see how we'd give !!a{derivation} of the !!{formula}
  103. $\lexists[x][!C(x,b)]$ from the assumptions $\lexists[x][(!A(x)
  104. \land !B(x))]$ and $\lforall[x][(!B(x) \lif !C(x,b))]$.
  105. Starting as usual, we write the conclusion at the
  106. bottom.
  107. \begin{prooftree}
  108. \AxiomC{}
  109. \UnaryInfC{$\lexists[x][!C(x,b)]$}
  110. \end{prooftree}
  112. We have two premises to work with. To use the first, i.e., try to find
  113. !!a{derivation} of $\lexists[x][!C(x, b)]$ from $\lexists[x][(!A(x)
  114. \land !B(x))]$ we would use the \Elim{\lexists} rule. Since it has
  115. an eigenvariable condition, we will apply that rule first. We get the
  116. following:
  117. \begin{prooftree}
  118. \AxiomC{$\lexists[x][(!A(x) \land !B(x))]$}
  119. \AxiomC{$\Discharge{!A(a) \land !B(a)}{1}$}
  120. \DeduceC{$\lexists[x][!C(x,b)]$}
  121. \DischargeRule{\Elim{\lexists}}{1}
  122. \BinaryInfC{$\lexists[x][!C(x,b)]$}
  123. \end{prooftree}
  124. The two assumptions we are working with share~$!B$. It may be useful
  125. at this point to apply \Elim{\land} to separate out~$!B(a)$.
  126. \begin{prooftree}
  127. \AxiomC{$\lexists[x][(!A(x) \land !B(x)])$}
  128. \AxiomC{$\Discharge{!A(a)
  129. \land !B(a)}{1}$}
  130. \RightLabel{\Elim{\land}}
  131. \UnaryInfC{$!B(a)$}
  132. \DeduceC{$\lexists[x][!C(x,b)]$}
  133. \DischargeRule{\Elim{\lexists}}{1}
  134. \BinaryInfC{$\lexists[x][!C(x,b)]$}
  135. \end{prooftree}
  137. The second assumption we have to work with is~$\lforall[x][(!B(x) \lif
  138. !C(x,b))]$. Since there is no eigenvariable condition we can
  139. instantiate $x$ with the !!{constant}~$a$ using \Elim{\lforall} to get
  140. $!B(a) \lif !C(a, b)$. We now have both $!B(a) \lif !C(a,b)$ and
  141. $!B(a)$. Our next move should be a straightforward application of the
  142. \Elim{\lif} rule.
  143. \begin{prooftree}
  144. \AxiomC{$\lexists[x][(!A(x) \land !B(x))]$}
  145. \AxiomC{$\lforall[x][(!B(x) \lif !C(x,b))]$}
  146. \RightLabel{\Elim{\lforall}}
  147. \UnaryInfC{$!B(a) \lif !C(a,b)$}
  148. \AxiomC{$\Discharge{!A(a)
  149. \land !B(a)}{1}$}
  150. \RightLabel{\Elim{\land}}
  151. \UnaryInfC{$!B(a)$}
  152. \RightLabel{\Elim{\lif}}
  153. \BinaryInfC{$!C(a,b)$}
  154. \DeduceC{$\lexists[x][!C(x,b)]$}
  155. \DischargeRule{\Elim{\lexists}}{1}
  156. \insertBetweenHyps{\hspace{-5em}}
  157. \BinaryInfC{$\lexists[x][!C(x,b)]$}
  158. \end{prooftree}
  159. We are so close!{} One application of \Intro{\lexists} and we
  160. have reached our goal.
  161. \begin{prooftree}
  162. \AxiomC{$\lexists[x][(!A(x) \land !B(x))]$}
  163. \AxiomC{$\lforall[x][(!B(x) \lif !C(x,b))]$}
  164. \RightLabel{\Elim{\lforall}}
  165. \UnaryInfC{$!B(a) \lif !C(a,b)$}
  166. \AxiomC{$\Discharge{!A(a)
  167. \land !B(a)}{1}$}
  168. \RightLabel{\Elim{\land}}
  169. \UnaryInfC{$!B(a)$}
  170. \RightLabel{\Elim{\lif}}
  171. \BinaryInfC{$!C(a,b)$}
  172. \RightLabel{\Intro{\lexists}}
  173. \UnaryInfC{$\lexists[x][!C(x,b)]$}
  174. \DischargeRule{\Elim{\lexists}}{1}
  175. \insertBetweenHyps{\hspace{-5em}}
  176. \BinaryInfC{$\lexists[x][!C(x,b)]$}
  177. \end{prooftree}
  178. Since we ensured at each step that the eigenvariable
  179. conditions were not violated, we can be confident that this
  180. is a correct !!{derivation}.
  181. \end{ex}
  183. \begin{ex}
  184. Give !!a{derivation} of the !!{formula}
  185. $\lnot\lforall[x][!A(x)]$ from the assumptions $\lforall[x][!A(x)]
  186. \lif \lexists[y][!B(y)]$ and $\lnot\lexists[y][!B(y)]$.

  92. \UnaryInfC{$\lforall[y][((!A(a) \land !A(y)) \lif \eq[a][y])]$}
  93. \RightLabel{\Intro{\lforall}}
  94. \UnaryInfC{$\lforall[x][\lforall[y][((!A(x) \land !A(y)) \lif \eq[x][y])]]$}
  95. \end{prooftree}
  96. The sub-!!{derivation} on the top right is completed by using its
  97. assumptions to show that $\eq[a][c]$ and $\eq[b][c]$. This requires two
  98. separate !!{derivation}s. The !!{derivation} for $\eq[a][c]$ is as
  99. follows:
  100. \begin{prooftree}
  101. \AxiomC{$\Discharge{\lforall[y][(!A(y) \lif \eq[y][c]])}{2}$}
  102. \RightLabel{\Elim{\lforall}}
  103. \UnaryInfC{$!A(a) \lif \eq[a][c]$}
  104. \AxiomC{$\Discharge{!A(a) \land !A(b)}{1}$}
  105. \RightLabel{\Elim{\land}}
  106. \UnaryInfC{$!A(a)$}
  107. \RightLabel{\Elim{\lif}}
  108. \BinaryInfC{$\eq[a][c]$}
  109. \end{prooftree}
  110. From $\eq[a][c]$ and $\eq[b][c]$ we !!{derive} $\eq[a][b]$ by
  111. \Elim{\eq}.
  112. \end{ex}

  13. \begin{prop}
  14. Natural deduction with rules for $\eq$ is sound.
  15. \end{prop}
  17. \begin{proof}
  18. Any !!{formula} of the form $\eq[t][t]$ is valid, since
  19. for every !!{structure}~$\Struct M$, $\Sat{M}{\eq[t][t]}$. (Note that
  20. we assume the term $t$ to be closed, i.e., it contains no variables,
  21. so variable assignments are irrelevant).
  23. Suppose the last inference in !!a{derivation} is \Elim{\eq}, i.e., the
  24. !!{derivation} has the following form:
  25. \begin{prooftree}
  26. \AxiomC{$\Gamma_1$}
  27. \RightLabel{$\delta_1$}
  28. \DeduceC{$\eq[t_1][t_2]$}
  29. \AxiomC{$\Gamma_2$}
  30. \RightLabel{$\delta_2$}
  31. \DeduceC{$!A(t_1)$}
  32. \RightLabel{\Elim{\eq}}
  33. \BinaryInfC{$!A(t_2)$}
  34. \end{prooftree}
  35. The premises $\eq[t_1][t_2]$ and $!A(t_1)$ are !!{derive}d from
  36. !!{undischarged} assumptions~$\Gamma_1$ and $\Gamma_2$, respectively.
  37. We want to show that $!A(t_2)$ follows from $\Gamma_1 \cup \Gamma_2$.
  38. Consider !!a{structure}~$\Struct{M}$ with $\Sat{M}{\Gamma_1 \cup
  39. \Gamma_2}$. By induction hypothesis, $\Sat{M}{!A(t_1)}$ and
  40. $\Sat{M}{\eq[t_1][t_2]}$. Therefore, $\Value{t_1}{M} = \Value{t_2}{M}$. Let
  41. $s$ be any variable assignment, and $m = \Value{t_1}{M} = \Value{t_2}{M}$. By
  42. \olref[fol][syn][ext]{prop:ext-formulas}, $\Sat{M}{!A(t_1)}[s]$ iff
  43. $\Sat{M}{!A(x)}[\Subst{s}{m}{x}]$ iff $\Sat{M}{!A(t_2)}[s]$. Since
  44. $\Sat{M}{!A(t_1)}$, we have $\Sat{M}{!A(t_2)}$.
  45. \end{proof}
