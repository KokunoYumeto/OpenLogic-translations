Symbols, pronunciation, and meaning

These are conventional contextual readings, not claims that mathematical notation has only one possible pronunciation.

Expression 1: assumption A, labeled n for discharge

[A]n[A]^nassumption A, labeled n for discharge

Read as: assumption A, labeled n for discharge

Means: An occurrence of assumption A marked with discharge label n; an inference bearing n may discharge it.

Why this reading: The superscript is an assumption-discharge label, not exponentiation or a proof-step number.
