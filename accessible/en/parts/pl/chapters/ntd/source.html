  13. \olsection{Rules and \usetoken{P}{derivation}}
  15. \begin{explain}
  16. Natural deduction systems are meant to closely parallel the informal
  17. reasoning used in mathematical proof (hence it is somewhat
  18. ``natural''). Natural deduction proofs begin with assumptions.
  19. Inference rules are then applied. Assumptions are ``!!{discharged}''
  20. by the \Intro{\lnot}, \Intro{\lif}, \iftag{FOL}{\Elim{\lor} and
  21. \Elim{\lexists}}{ and \Elim{\lor}} inference rules, and the label of
  22. the !!{discharged} assumption is placed beside the inference for
  23. clarity.
  24. \end{explain}
  26. \begin{defn}[Assumption]
  27. An \emph{assumption} is any !!{sentence}
  28. in the topmost position of any branch.
  29. \end{defn}
  31. !!^{derivation}s in natural deduction are certain trees of
  32. !!{sentence}s, where the topmost !!{sentence}s are assumptions, and if
  33. !!a{sentence} stands below one, two, or three other sequents, it must
  34. follow correctly by a rule of inference. The !!{sentence}s at the top
  35. of the inference are called the \emph{premises} and the !!{sentence}
  36. below the \emph{conclusion} of the inference. The rules come in
  37. pairs, an introduction and an elimination rule for each
  38. !!{operator}. They introduce !!a{operator} in the conclusion or
  39. remove !!a{operator} from a premise of the rule. Some of the rules
  40. allow an assumption of a certain type to be \emph{!!{discharged}}. To
  41. indicate which assumption is !!{discharged} by which inference, we
  42. also assign labels to both the assumption and the inference. This is
  43. indicated by writing the assumption as ``$\Discharge{!A}{n}$.''
  45. % Only include this sentence is on of \land, lor, lif or lnot is defined.
  47. \iftag{notprvNot,notprvAnd,notprvOr,notprvIf}{}%
  48. {It is customary to consider rules for all the !!{operator}s $\land$, $\lor$, $\lif$, $\lnot$, and $\lfalse$, even if some of those are defined.}

  13. \olsection{\usetoken{P}{derivation}}
  15. \begin{explain}
  16. We've said what an assumption is, and we've given the rules of
  17. inference. !!^{derivation}s in natural deduction are inductively
  18. generated from these: each !!{derivation} either is an assumption
  19. on its own, or consists of one, two, or three !!{derivation}s followed
  20. by a correct inference.
  21. \end{explain}
  23. \begin{defn}[!!^{derivation}]
  24. \Article{derivation} \emph{!!{derivation}} of !!a{sentence}~$!A$ from
  25. assumptions~$\Gamma$ is a finite tree of !!{sentence}s satisfying the
  26. following conditions:
  27. \begin{enumerate}
  28. \item The topmost !!{sentence}s of the tree are either in $\Gamma$ or
  29. are !!{discharged} by an inference in the tree.
  30. \item The bottommost !!{sentence} of the tree is~$!A$.
  31. \item Every !!{sentence} in the tree except the sentence~$!A$ at
  32. the bottom is a premise of a correct application of an inference
  33. rule whose conclusion stands directly below that !!{sentence} in the
  34. tree.
  35. \end{enumerate}
  36. We then say that $!A$ is the \emph{conclusion} of the !!{derivation}
  37. and $\Gamma$ its !!{undischarged} assumptions.
  39. If !!a{derivation} of $!A$ from~$\Gamma$ exists, we say that $!A$ is
  40. \emph{!!{derivable}} from~$\Gamma$, or in symbols: $\Gamma \Proves
  41. !A$. If there is !!a{derivation} of~$!A$ in which every assumption is
  42. !!{discharged}, we write~$\Proves !A$.
  43. \end{defn}
  45. \begin{ex}
  46. Every assumption on its own is !!a{derivation}. So, e.g., $!A$ by
  47. itself is !!a{derivation}, and so is $!B$ by itself. We can obtain a
  48. new !!{derivation} from these by applying, say, the $\Intro{\land}$
  49. rule,
  50. \begin{prooftree}
  51. \AxiomC{$!A$}
  52. \AxiomC{$!B$}
  53. \RightLabel{\Intro{\land}}
  54. \BinaryInfC{$!A \land !B$}
  55. \end{prooftree}
  56. These rules are meant to be general: we can replace the $!A$ and~$!B$
  57. in it with any !!{sentence}s, e.g., by $!C$ and~$!D$. Then the
  58. conclusion would be $!C \land !D$, and so
  59. \begin{prooftree}
  60. \AxiomC{$!C$}
  61. \AxiomC{$!D$}
  62. \RightLabel{\Intro{\land}}
  63. \BinaryInfC{$!C \land !D$}
  64. \end{prooftree}
  65. is a correct !!{derivation}. Of course, we can also switch the
  66. assumptions, so that $!D$ plays the role of~$!A$ and $!C$ that
  67. of~$!B$. Thus,
  68. \begin{prooftree}
  69. \AxiomC{$!D$}
  70. \AxiomC{$!C$}
  71. \RightLabel{\Intro{\land}}
  72. \BinaryInfC{$!D \land !C$}
  73. \end{prooftree}
  74. is also a correct !!{derivation}.
  76. We can now apply another rule, say, $\Intro{\lif}$, which allows us to
  77. conclude a conditional and allows us to !!{discharge} any assumption
  78. that is identical to the antecedent of that conditional. So both of
  79. the following would be correct !!{derivation}s:
  80. \begin{prooftree}
  81. \AxiomC{$\Discharge{!C}{1}$}
  82. \AxiomC{$!D$}
  83. \RightLabel{\Intro{\land}}
  84. \BinaryInfC{$!C \land !D$}
  85. \DischargeRule{\Intro{\lif}}{1}
  86. \UnaryInfC{$!C \lif (!C \land !D)$}
  87. \DisplayProof\bottomAlignProof
  88. \AxiomC{$!C$}
  89. \AxiomC{$\Discharge{!D}{1}$}
  90. \RightLabel{\Intro{\land}}
  91. \BinaryInfC{$!C \land !D$}
  92. \DischargeRule{\Intro{\lif}}{1}
  93. \UnaryInfC{$!D \lif (!C \land !D)$}
  94. \end{prooftree}
  95. They show, respectively, that $!D \Proves !C \lif (!C \land !D)$ and
  96. $!C \Proves !D \lif (!C \land !D)$.
  98. Remember that discharging of assumptions is a permission, not a
  99. requirement: we don't have to discharge the assumptions. In
  100. particular, we can apply a rule even if the assumptions are not
  101. present in the !!{derivation}. For instance, the following is legal, even
  102. though there is no assumption~$!A$ to be !!{discharged}:
  103. \begin{prooftree}
  104. \AxiomC{$!B$}
  105. \DischargeRule{\Intro{\lif}}{1}
  106. \UnaryInfC{$!A \lif !B$}
  107. \end{prooftree}
  108. \end{ex}
  110. \end{document}

  29. the assumptions involved and label the inference rules as you
  30. progress, so it is easy to see whether all assumptions have been
  31. !!{discharged} at the end of the proof.

  33. \AxiomC{$\Discharge{!A \land !B}{1}$}

  43. \AxiomC{$\Discharge{!A \land !B}{1}$}
  44. \RightLabel{\Elim{\land}}

  64. look at the logical connectives in the conclusion: $\lnot$,

  179. So far we have not needed the \FalseCl{} rule. It is special in that
  180. it allows us to discharge an assumption that isn't a sub-!!{formula} of

  182. rule. In fact, the \FalseInt{} rule is a special case of the
  183. \FalseCl{} rule---there is a logic called ``intuitionistic logic'' in
  184. which only \FalseInt{} is allowed. The \FalseCl{} rule is a last
  185. resort when nothing else works. For instance, suppose we want to
  186. !!{derive} $!A \lor \lnot !A$. Our usual strategy would be to attempt
  187. to !!{derive} $!A \lor \lnot !A$ using $\Intro{\lor}$. But this would
  188. require us to !!{derive} either $!A$ or $\lnot !A$ from no

  98. \item \ollabel{prop:provability-lif-right}
  99. Both $\lnot !A \Proves !A \lif !B$ and $!B \Proves !A \lif !B$.
  100. \end{enumerate}
  101. \end{prop}
  103. \begin{proof}
  104. \begin{enumerate}
  105. \item We can !!{derive}:
  106. \begin{prooftree}
  107. \AxiomC{$!A \lif !B$}
  108. \AxiomC{$!A$}
  109. \RightLabel{\Elim{\lif}}
  110. \BinaryInfC{$!B$}
  111. \end{prooftree}
  113. \item This is shown by the following two !!{derivation}s:
  114. \begin{prooftree}
  115. \AxiomC{$\lnot !A$}
  116. \AxiomC{$\Discharge{!A}{1}$}
  117. \RightLabel{\Elim{\lnot}}
  118. \BinaryInfC{$\lfalse$}
  119. \RightLabel{\FalseInt}
  120. \UnaryInfC{$!B$}
  121. \DischargeRule{\Intro{\lif}}{1}
  122. \UnaryInfC{$!A \lif !B$}
  123. \DisplayProof\qquad\bottomAlignProof
  124. \AxiomC{$!B$}
  125. \RightLabel{\Intro{\lif}}
  126. \UnaryInfC{$!A \lif !B$}
  127. \end{prooftree}
  128. Note that $\Intro{\lif}$ may, but does not have to, !!{discharge} the
  129. assumption~$!A$.
  130. \end{enumerate}
  131. \end{proof}

  13. \olsection{Soundness}
  15. \begin{explain}
  16. !!^a{derivation} system, such as natural deduction, is \emph{sound}
  17. if it cannot !!{derive} things that do not actually follow. Soundness is
  18. thus a kind of guaranteed safety property for !!{derivation} systems.
  19. Depending on which proof theoretic property is in question, we would
  20. like to know for instance, that
  21. \begin{enumerate}
  22. \item every !!{derivable} !!{sentence} is \iftag{FOL}{valid}{a tautology};
  23. \item if !!a{sentence} is !!{derivable} from some others, it is also a
  24. consequence of them;
  25. \item if a set of !!{sentence}s is inconsistent, it is unsatisfiable.
  26. \end{enumerate}
  27. These are important properties of !!a{derivation} system. If any of
  28. them do not hold, the !!{derivation} system is deficient---it would
  29. !!{derive} too much. Consequently, establishing the soundness of a
  30. !!{derivation} system is of the utmost importance.
  31. \end{explain}
  33. \begin{thm}[Soundness]
  34. \ollabel{thm:soundness}
  35. If $!A$ is !!{derivable} from the !!{undischarged} assumptions
  36. $\Gamma$, then $\Gamma \Entails !A$.
  37. \end{thm}
  39. \begin{proof}
  40. Let $\delta$ be !!a{derivation} of $!A$. We proceed by
  41. induction on the number of inferences in~$\delta$.
  43. For the induction basis we show the claim if the number of inferences
  44. is~$0$. In this case, $\delta$ consists only of a single
  45. !!{sentence}~$!A$, i.e., an assumption. That assumption is
  46. !!{undischarged}, since assumptions can only be !!{discharged} by
  47. inferences, and there are no inferences. So, any
  48. \iftag{FOL}{!!{structure}~$\Struct{M}$}{!!{valuation}~$\pAssign{v}$}
  49. that satisfies all of the !!{undischarged} assumptions of the proof
  50. also satisfies~$!A$.
  52. Now for the inductive step. Suppose that $\delta$ contains~$n$
  53. inferences. The premise(s) of the lowermost inference are !!{derive}d
  54. using sub-!!{derivation}s, each of which contains fewer than~$n$
  55. inferences. We assume the induction hypothesis: The premises of the
  56. lowermost inference follow from the !!{undischarged} assumptions of
  57. the sub-!!{derivation}s ending in those premises. We have to show
  58. that the conclusion~$!A$ follows from the !!{undischarged} assumptions
  59. of the entire proof.
  61. We distinguish cases according to the type of the lowermost inference.
  62. First, we consider the possible inferences with only one premise.
  64. \begin{enumerate}
  65. \item Suppose that the last inference is \Intro{\lnot}: The
  66. !!{derivation} has the form
  67. \begin{prooftree}
  68. \AxiomC{$\Gamma, \Discharge{!A}{n}$}
  69. \RightLabel{$\delta_1$}
  70. \DeduceC{$\lfalse$}
  71. \DischargeRule{\Intro{\lnot}}{n}
  72. \UnaryInfC{$\lnot !A$}
  73. \end{prooftree}
  74. By inductive hypothesis, $\lfalse$ follows from the !!{undischarged}
  75. assumptions $\Gamma \cup \{!A\}$ of~$\delta_1$. Consider
  76. \iftag{FOL}{!!a{structure}~$\Struct{M}$}{!!a{valuation}~$\pAssign{v}$}. We
  77. need to show that, if $\iftag{FOL}{\Sat{M}}{\pSat{v}}{\Gamma}$, then
  78. $\iftag{FOL}{\Sat{M}}{\pSat{v}}{\lnot !A}$. Suppose for reductio
  79. that $\iftag{FOL}{\Sat{M}}{\pSat{v}}{\Gamma}$, but
  80. $\iftag{FOL}{\Sat/{M}}{\pSat/{v}}{\lnot !A}$, i.e.,
  81. $\iftag{FOL}{\Sat{M}}{\pSat{v}}{!A}$. This would mean that
  82. $\iftag{FOL}{\Sat{M}}{\pSat{v}}{\Gamma \cup \{!A\}}$. This is
  83. contrary to our inductive hypothesis. So,
  84. $\iftag{FOL}{\Sat{M}}{\pSat{v}}{\lnot !A}$.
  86. \item The last inference is \Elim{\land}: There are two variants: $!A$
  87. or $!B$ may be inferred from the premise $!A \land !B$. Consider the
  88. first case. The !!{derivation}~$\delta$ looks like this:
  89. \begin{prooftree}
  90. \AxiomC{$\Gamma$}
  91. \RightLabel{$\delta_1$}
  92. \DeduceC{$!A \land !B$}
  93. \RightLabel{\Elim{\land}}
  94. \UnaryInfC{$!A$}
  95. \end{prooftree}
  96. By inductive hypothesis, $!A \land !B$ follows from the
  97. !!{undischarged} assumptions~$\Gamma$ of~$\delta_1$. Consider
  98. !!a{structure}~\iftag{FOL}{$\Struct{M}$}{$\pAssign{v}$}. We need to
  99. show that, if $\iftag{FOL}{\Sat{M}}{\pSat{v}}{\Gamma}$, then
  100. $\iftag{FOL}{\Sat{M}}{\pSat{v}}{!A}$. Suppose
  101. $\iftag{FOL}{\Sat{M}}{\pSat{v}}{\Gamma}$. By our inductive
  102. hypothesis ($\Gamma \Entails !A \land !B$), we know that
  103. $\iftag{FOL}{\Sat{M}}{\pSat{v}}{!A \land !B}$. By definition,
  104. $\iftag{FOL}{\Sat{M}}{\pSat{v}}{!A \land !B}$ iff
  105. $\iftag{FOL}{\Sat{M}}{\pSat{v}}{!A}$ and
  106. $\iftag{FOL}{\Sat{M}}{\pSat{v}}{!B}$. (The case where $!B$ is
  107. inferred from $!A \land !B$ is handled similarly.)
  109. \item The last inference is \Intro{\lor}: There are two variants: $!A
  110. \lor !B$ may be inferred from the premise~$!A$ or the
  111. premise~$!B$. Consider the first case. The !!{derivation} has the form
  112. \begin{prooftree}
  113. \AxiomC{$\Gamma$}
  114. \RightLabel{$\delta_1$}
  115. \DeduceC{$!A$}
  116. \RightLabel{\Intro{\lor}}
  117. \UnaryInfC{$!A \lor !B$}
  118. \end{prooftree}
  119. By inductive hypothesis, $!A$ follows from the !!{undischarged}
  120. assumptions~$\Gamma$ of~$\delta_1$. Consider
  121. \iftag{FOL}{!!a{structure}~$\Struct{M}$}{!!a{valuation}~$\pAssign{v}$}. We
  122. need to show that, if $\iftag{FOL}{\Sat{M}}{\pSat{v}}{\Gamma}$, then
  123. $\iftag{FOL}{\Sat{M}}{\pSat{v}}{!A \lor !B}$. Suppose
  124. $\iftag{FOL}{\Sat{M}}{\pSat{v}}{\Gamma}$; then
  125. $\iftag{FOL}{\Sat{M}}{\pSat{v}}{!A}$ since $\Gamma \Entails !A$ (the
  126. inductive hypothesis). So it must also be the case that
  127. $\iftag{FOL}{\Sat{M}}{\pSat{v}}{!A \lor !B}$. (The case where $!A
  128. \lor !B$ is inferred from~$!B$ is handled similarly.)
  130. \item The last inference is \Intro{\lif}: $!A \lif !B$ is inferred
  131. from a subproof with assumption~$!A$ and conclusion~$!B$, i.e.,
  132. \begin{prooftree}
  133. \AxiomC{$\Gamma, \Discharge{!A}{n}$}
  134. \RightLabel{$\delta_1$}
  135. \DeduceC{$!B$}
  136. \DischargeRule{\Intro{\lif}}{n}
  137. \UnaryInfC{$!A \lif !B$}
  138. \end{prooftree}
  139. By inductive hypothesis, $!B$ follows from the !!{undischarged}
  140. assumptions of~$\delta_1$, i.e., $\Gamma \cup \{!A\} \Entails
  141. !B$. Consider
  142. \iftag{FOL}{!!a{structure}~$\Struct{M}$}{!!a{valuation}~$\pAssign{v}$}. The
  143. !!{undischarged} assumptions of~$\delta$ are just $\Gamma$, since
  144. $!A$ is discharged at the last inference. So we need to show that
  145. $\Gamma \Entails !A \lif !B$. For reductio, suppose that for some
  146. \iftag{FOL}{!!{structure}~$\Struct{M}$}{!!{valuation}~$\pAssign{v}$},
  147. $\iftag{FOL}{\Sat{M}}{\pSat{v}}{\Gamma}$ but
  148. $\iftag{FOL}{\Sat/{M}}{\pSat/{v}}{!A \lif !B}$. So,
  149. $\iftag{FOL}{\Sat{M}}{\pSat{v}}{!A}$ and
  150. $\iftag{FOL}{\Sat/{M}}{\pSat/{v}}{!B}$. But by hypothesis, $!B$ is a
  151. consequence of $\Gamma \cup \{!A\}$, i.e.,
  152. $\iftag{FOL}{\Sat{M}}{\pSat{v}}{!B}$, which is a contradiction. So,
  153. $\Gamma \Entails !A \lif !B$.
  155. \item The last inference is \FalseInt: Here, $\delta$ ends in
  156. \begin{prooftree}
  157. \AxiomC{$\Gamma$}
  158. \RightLabel{$\delta_1$}
  159. \DeduceC{$\lfalse$}
  160. \RightLabel{\FalseInt}
  161. \UnaryInfC{$!A$}
  162. \end{prooftree}
  163. By induction hypothesis, $\Gamma \Entails \lfalse$. We have to show
  164. that $\Gamma \Entails !A$. Suppose not; then for
  165. some~$\iftag{FOL}{\Struct{M}}{\pAssign{v}}$ we have
  166. $\iftag{FOL}{\Sat{M}}{\pSat{v}}{\Gamma}$ and
  167. $\iftag{FOL}{\Sat/{M}}{\pSat/{v}}{!A}$. But we always have
  168. $\iftag{FOL}{\Sat/{M}}{\pSat/{v}}{\lfalse}$, so this would mean
  169. that $\Gamma \Entails/ \lfalse$, contrary to the induction
  170. hypothesis.
  172. \item The last inference is \FalseCl: Exercise.
  174. \iftag{FOL}{
  175. \item The last inference is \Intro{\lforall}: Then $\delta$ has the form
  176. \begin{prooftree}
  177. \AxiomC{$\Gamma$}
  178. \RightLabel{$\delta_1$}
  179. \DeduceC{$!A(a)$}
  180. \RightLabel{\Intro{\lforall}}
  181. \UnaryInfC{$\lforall[x][!A(x)]$}
  182. \end{prooftree}
  183. The premise $!A(a)$ is a consequence of the !!{undischarged}
  184. assumptions $\Gamma$ by induction hypothesis. Consider some
  185. structure, $\Struct{M}$, such that $\Sat{M}{\Gamma}$. We need to
  186. show that $\Sat{M}{\lforall[x][!A(x)]}$. Since $\lforall[x][!A(x)]$
  187. is !!a{sentence}, this means we have to show that for every variable
  188. assignment~$s$, $\Sat{M}{!A(x)}[s]$
  189. (\olref[syn][ass]{prop:sat-quant}). Since $\Gamma$ consists entirely
  190. of sentences, $\Sat{M}{!B}[s]$ for all $!B \in \Gamma$ by
  191. \olref[syn][sat]{defn:satisfaction}. Let $\Struct{M'}$ be like
  192. $\Struct{M}$ except that $\Assign{a}{M'} = s(x)$. Since $a$ does
  193. not occur in~$\Gamma$, $\Sat{M'}{\Gamma}$ by
  194. \olref[syn][ext]{cor:extensionality-sent}. Since $\Gamma \Entails
  195. !A(a)$, $\Sat{M'}{!A(a)}$. Since $!A(a)$ is !!a{sentence},
  196. $\Sat{M'}{!A(a)}[s]$ by
  197. \olref[syn][ass]{prop:sentence-sat-true}. $\Sat{M'}{!A(x)}[s]$ iff
  198. $\Sat{M'}{!A(a)}$ by \olref[syn][ext]{prop:ext-formulas} (recall
  199. that $!A(a)$ is just $\Subst{!A(x)}{a}{x}$). So,
  200. $\Sat{M'}{!A(x)}[s]$. Since $a$ does not occur in~$!A(x)$, by
  201. \olref[syn][ext]{prop:extensionality}, $\Sat{M}{!A(x)}[s]$. But $s$
  202. was an arbitrary variable assignment, so
  203. $\Sat{M}{\lforall[x][!A(x)]}$.
  205. \item The last inference is \Intro{\lexists}: Exercise.
  207. \item The last inference is \Elim{\forall}: Exercise.
  208. }{}
  209. \end{enumerate}
  211. Now let's consider the possible inferences with several premises:
  212. \Elim{\lor}, \Intro{\land}, \iftag{FOL}{\Elim{\lif}, and
  213. \Elim{\lexists}}{and \Elim{\lif}}.
  214. \begin{enumerate}
  216. \item The last inference is \Intro{\land}. $!A \land !B$ is inferred
  217. from the premises $!A$ and $!B$ and $\delta$ has the form
  218. \begin{prooftree}
  219. \AxiomC{$\Gamma_1$}
  220. \RightLabel{$\delta_1$}
  221. \DeduceC{$!A$}
  222. \AxiomC{$\Gamma_2$}
  223. \RightLabel{$\delta_2$}
  224. \DeduceC{$!B$}
  225. \RightLabel{\Intro{\land}}
  226. \BinaryInfC{$!A \land !B$}
  227. \end{prooftree}
  228. By induction hypothesis, $!A$ follows from the !!{undischarged}
  229. assumptions~$\Gamma_1$ of~$\delta_1$ and $!B$ follows from the
  230. !!{undischarged} assumptions~$\Gamma_2$ of~$\delta_2$. The
  231. !!{undischarged} assumptions of~$\delta$ are $\Gamma_1 \cup
  232. \Gamma_2$, so we have to show that $\Gamma_1 \cup \Gamma_2 \Entails
  233. !A \land !B$. Consider
  234. \iftag{FOL}{!!a{structure}~$\Struct{M}$}{!!a{valuation}~$\pAssign{v}$}
  235. with $\iftag{FOL}{\Sat{M}}{\pSat{v}}{\Gamma_1 \cup \Gamma_2}$. Since
  236. $\iftag{FOL}{\Sat{M}}{\pSat{v}}{\Gamma_1}$, it must be the case that
  237. $\iftag{FOL}{\Sat{M}}{\pSat{v}}{!A}$ as $\Gamma_1 \Entails !A$, and
  238. since $\iftag{FOL}{\Sat{M}}{\pSat{v}}{\Gamma_2}$,
  239. $\iftag{FOL}{\Sat{M}}{\pSat{v}}{!B}$ since $\Gamma_2 \Entails
  240. !B$. Together, $\iftag{FOL}{\Sat{M}}{\pSat{v}}{!A \land !B}$.
  242. \item The last inference is \Elim{\lor}: Exercise.
  244. \item The last inference is \Elim{\lif}. $!B$ is inferred from the
  245. premises $!A \lif !B$ and~$!A$. The !!{derivation}~$\delta$ looks like this:
  246. \begin{prooftree}
  247. \AxiomC{$\Gamma_1$}
  248. \RightLabel{$\delta_1$}
  249. \DeduceC{$!A \lif !B$}
  250. \AxiomC{$\Gamma_2$}
  251. \RightLabel{$\delta_2$}
  252. \DeduceC{$!A$}
  253. \RightLabel{\Elim{\lif}}
  254. \BinaryInfC{$!B$}
  255. \end{prooftree}
  256. By induction hypothesis, $!A \lif !B$ follows from the
  257. !!{undischarged} assumptions~$\Gamma_1$ of~$\delta_1$ and $!A$
  258. follows from the !!{undischarged} assumptions~$\Gamma_2$
  259. of~$\delta_2$. Consider
  260. \iftag{FOL}{!!a{structure}~$\Struct{M}$}{!!a{valuation}~$\pAssign{v}$}. We
  261. need to show that, if $\iftag{FOL}{\Sat{M}}{\pSat{v}}{\Gamma_1 \cup
  262. \Gamma_2}$, then $\iftag{FOL}{\Sat{M}}{\pSat{v}}{!B}$. Suppose
  263. $\iftag{FOL}{\Sat{M}}{\pSat{v}}{\Gamma_1 \cup \Gamma_2}$. Since
  264. $\Gamma_1 \Entails !A \lif !B$, $\iftag{FOL}{\Sat{M}}{\pSat{v}}{!A
  265. \lif !B}$. Since $\Gamma_2 \Entails !A$, we have
  266. $\iftag{FOL}{\Sat{M}}{\pSat{v}}{!A}$. This means that
  267. $\iftag{FOL}{\Sat{M}}{\pSat{v}}{!B}$ (For if
  268. $\iftag{FOL}{\Sat/{M}}{\pSat/{v}}{!B}$, since
  269. $\iftag{FOL}{\Sat{M}}{\pSat{v}}{!A}$, we'd have
  270. $\iftag{FOL}{\Sat/{M}}{\pSat/{v}}{!A \lif !B}$,
  271. contradicting~$\iftag{FOL}{\Sat{M}}{\pSat{v}}{!A \lif !B}$).
  273. \item The last inference is \Elim{\lnot}: Exercise.

  276. \end{enumerate}

  292. \ollabel{cor:weak-soundness}
  293. If $\Proves !A$, then $!A$ is \iftag{FOL}{valid}{a tautology}.

  296. \begin{cor}
  297. \ollabel{cor:consistency-soundness}
  298. If $\Gamma$ is satisfiable, then it is consistent.
  299. \end{cor}
  301. \begin{proof}
  302. We prove the contrapositive. Suppose that $\Gamma$ is not consistent.
  303. Then $\Gamma \Proves \lfalse$, i.e., there is !!a{derivation} of
  304. $\lfalse$ from !!{undischarged} assumptions in~$\Gamma$. By
  305. \olref{thm:soundness}, any
  306. \iftag{FOL}{!!{structure}~$\Struct{M}$}{!!{valuation}~$\pAssign{v}$}
  307. that satisfies $\Gamma$ must satisfy $\lfalse$. Since
  308. $\iftag{FOL}{\Sat/{M}}{\pSat/{v}}{\lfalse}$ for every
  309. \iftag{FOL}{!!{structure}~$\Struct{M}$}{!!{valuation}~$\pAssign{v}$},
  310. no \iftag{FOL}{$\Struct{M}$}{$\pAssign{v}$} can satisfy $\Gamma$,
  311. i.e., $\Gamma$ is not satisfiable.
  312. \end{proof}
